Most recent · built this week

RUYA, one home for a company's AI agents

Inside large companies, teams build the same AI agent again and again, in isolation, with no way to tell which one is best. RUYA brings them into one hub, tests every agent on the same examples, ranks them on a single board and sends the winner back into Copilot so everyone can use it.

  • ProblemTaken from a real enterprise brief: agent sprawl with no sharing and no proof of quality
  • MethodEvery agent scored on identical test sets, so the ranking is fair
  • RootsGrew out of MIZAN, a government AI model registry I built and pitched to TDRA

Gold Hedge

A gold and silver trading platform built to UAE compliance standards.

Buying and selling physical gold online sounds simple until you account for regulation, live institutional pricing and money that can never be off by a single fils. I built the whole platform: live prices from an institutional broker, customer onboarding, anti-money-laundering reporting, and a ledger that tracks every gram down to the milligram.

  • PricingLive connection to LMAX Global over the FIX 4.4 trading protocol
  • ComplianceBuilt around DMCC and DNFBP rules, with goAML reporting to the UAE FIU
  • PrecisionWeights held in milligrams, money in exact decimals, every transaction fully isolated
