    How it works

    From scattered files to a morning briefing

    STEP 1 · CONNECT

    Plug in what you already use

    Bank accounts, Excel ledgers, Google Ads, WhatsApp Business and the unit management system. Where a system has no API, Keel uses a custom secure pipeline.

    STEP 2 · UNDERSTAND

    One reconciled set of numbers

    Transactions are matched, categorised and checked for duplicates, then turned into the KPIs that matter for your industry: occupancy, REVPAF, move-ins, delinquency.

    STEP 3 · ACT

    Advice with the money attached

    Every morning Keel explains what changed and why, ranks it by impact in dirhams, and drafts the next step for you to approve.

    Proposed roadmap

    Win one vertical, then widen

    Vertical software beats general ERPs when it speaks the operator's language. Storage is the beachhead because we have a pilot operator and peers ready to test.

    Phase 1 · Q4 2026 to Q1 2027

    Self-storage pilot

    • Pilot with our own storage operation
    • Bank, Excel, Ads and unit-system connectors
    • Health score, briefing and Ask Keel
    • 5 to 10 peer operators on feedback loop
    Phase 2 · 2027

    Adjacent verticals

    • Warehousing and logistics KPI packs
    • Accounting connectors: Zoho Books, Xero, QuickBooks
    • WhatsApp collections workflows
    Phase 3 · 2027 onward

    The SME operating layer

    • CRM sync and two-way actions
    • Benchmarks against anonymised peers
    • Lender and investor reporting packs

    Security from day one

    Built to be trusted with a company's books

    Financial data raises the bar. The architecture plans for certification from the first line of code, not as a retrofit.

    Access

    Read-only connections by default. Keel drafts actions; a person approves them.

    Pipelines

    Custom parsers for systems without APIs, each sandboxed, validated and logged.

    Encryption

    Encrypted in transit and at rest, with per-tenant keys and data held in-region.

    Testing

    Independent penetration testing before any paying customer goes live.

    Certification

    Controls mapped to SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 from the start of the build.

    Audit trail

    Every number traces back to the transaction, row or event it came from.
